OUR ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

Why We Built FiberOps v4.0

Fast-growing Internet Service Providers and FTTH carriers shouldn't have to choose between agile field operations and rigorous cybersecurity. Yet most operators still stitch together chat apps, disconnected spreadsheets, manual MikroTik Winbox sessions, and paper handover receipts.

Benteng Siber closes that operational and security divide by treating every field action—from optical dBm verification and warehouse BOM deduction to Dynamic QRIS collection and MikroTik PPPoE restoration—as a deterministic, auditable, Zero-Trust transaction.
