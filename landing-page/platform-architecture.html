1. Strict Row-Level Multi-Tenant SaaS & 5-Role RBAC

Every ISP tenant registered on Benteng Siber operates inside an isolated logical boundary enforced at the Eloquent global scope and HTTP middleware layer. Every operational record—subscribers, GIS nodes (OLT/ODC/ODP), work orders, inventory materials, payment transactions, and technician GPS breadcrumbs—is permanently partitioned by tenant_id.

2. Active Network Orchestrator: MikroTik RouterOS v7 & OLT/GenieACS

Traditional FSM software stops at closing a ticket—forcing technicians to message NOC staff on WhatsApp to manually create PPPoE secrets, check fiber attenuation on the OLT, or lift customer isolation in Winbox. Benteng Siber eliminates that bottleneck with native driver abstractions:

  • MikroTik RouterOS v7 REST Driver (MikrotikRestDriver): Automatically provisions new PPPoE secrets upon installation, switches delinquent customers to the ISOLIR profile, and restores active bandwidth packages instantaneously when payment settles.
  • OLT / GenieACS TR-069 Optical Gate (GenieAcsDriver): Pulls real-time ONT Rx optical power (-dBm) directly from your ACS/OLT hardware. If fiber splice attenuation falls outside acceptable carrier tolerance (e.g., worse than -27.0 dBm), supervisors and quality gates immediately flag the ticket before BAST issuance.
  • Safe Simulation & Production Toggle: ISPs can test every workflow safely in Simulator Mode before switching their tenant driver to Production RouterOS REST or GenieACS inside encrypted tenant settings.

3. On-Site Dynamic QRIS & Async Queue Architecture

Field cash collection is one of the largest sources of revenue leakage in regional ISPs. Benteng Siber embeds live On-Site Dynamic QRIS directly inside the Technician Mobile PWA and Admin Console—coupled with non-blocking Laravel queue workers so third-party payment gateways never time out.

01

On-Site QR Generation

Technician taps Generate QRIS inside the ticket view. The server creates a unique invoice payload tied to the tenant's encrypted Midtrans credentials and displays the scannable QR code on the technician's screen.

02

Signed Webhook Verification (< 150 ms)

The subscriber pays via any Indonesian e-wallet or mobile banking app. Midtrans posts to the rate-limited webhook; Benteng Siber verifies the SHA-512 signature and exact gross_amount, marks the invoice settled atomically, and returns HTTP 200 immediately.

03

Async Hardware Execution (ShouldQueue)

UnIsolateCustomerOnPaymentJob runs asynchronously on the background queue worker (tries=3, exponential backoff 5s / 30s / 120s), calling the tenant's MikroTik RouterOS API to restore PPPoE access even if the ISP router has transient latency spikes.

04

3-Second Live Sync to PWA & NOC

Both the Technician PWA and Super Admin NOC dashboard receive live delta updates showing PAID & UN-ISOLATED—ready to seal directly into the customer's digital BAST certificate.

4. Deterministic Work Order State Machine & Atomic BOM

Every fiber ticket follows a strict, server-validated Finite State Machine: pending → assigned → en_route → in_progress → completed (with controlled rework or cancelled transitions).

Pessimistic Row-Level Concurrency: When a technician consumes drop-core cable, ONT units, or assigns a subscriber to an ODP port, Benteng Siber acquires MySQL row locks (lockForUpdate()) inside an atomic transaction. Two technicians arriving simultaneously can never oversubscribe an ODP port or drive warehouse stock negative.

5. Certified Public BAST Verification (/bast/verify/{hash})

Upon ticket completion, Benteng Siber computes a canonical SHA-256 cryptographic digest binding the task ID, completion timestamp, 3-slot photo proof references, optical power readings (-dBm), BOM materials, payment reference, and customer on-glass signature.

Every printed or PDF handover document embeds a public verification QR code linking to admin.bentengsiber.com/bast/verify/{hash}, allowing subscribers, corporate clients, and auditors to verify authenticity in one camera scan with zero login required.

6. Offline-First Technician PWA & Android APK Sync

Built on Ionic 8, Angular 20, and Capacitor 8, the field app is engineered for basements, rural fiber routes, and zero-signal splice enclosures:

  • Local Cache & FIFO Idempotent Queue: Status updates, BOM items, and proofs queue safely offline with unique X-Idempotency-Key headers preventing duplicate writes upon reconnection.
  • Batch GPS Flushing: Location breadcrumbs buffer locally and flush to /api/location/batch automatically.
  • 3-Second Lightweight Sync Signal: Ultra-compact delta polling keeps dispatch status, QRIS payment confirmation, and new work orders synchronized without draining battery or cellular quotas.
