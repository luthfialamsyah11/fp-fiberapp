Privacy Policy & Data Protection Standards

This policy explains how Benteng Siber collects, processes, protects, and retains data across our public website, FiberOps Field Service Management ecosystem, and security posture tooling.

1. Scope & Applicability

This policy applies to all live services hosted under bentengsiber.com and its active operational portals (admin.bentengsiber.com and app.bentengsiber.com).

2. FiberOps Telemetry & Device Permissions

Within the FiberOps Field Technician PWA and native Android client, device permissions are requested strictly for field execution:

  • Location (GPS): Collected during active work order dispatch for live NOC fleet routing, offline batch queue sync, and subscriber arrival estimates.
  • Camera & Local Storage: Used exclusively to capture guided Before, After, and Additional proof-of-work photographs with optical power (dBm) metadata and offline task caching.
  • Customer E-Signature: Bound to work order completion records and SHA-256 BAST cryptographic integrity verification.

3. Encryption & Access Controls

All production traffic is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS with HSTS enabled. Backend REST APIs enforce token-based authentication (Laravel Sanctum), brute-force login rate limiting, role-based access control (Super Admin and Field Technician), anti-IDOR ownership checks, and idempotency keys on mutating requests.

4. Public Service Request & Tracking Privacy

Service requests submitted through admin.bentengsiber.com/request and subscriber tracking lookups at admin.bentengsiber.com/track are protected with rate limiting, phone-digit verification, automatic 24-hour post-completion token expiry, and redaction of private technician contact numbers.

5. Passive Security Audit Policy

Security posture inspections analyze publicly accessible technical metadata only (HTTP security headers, X.509 TLS certificates, DNS/SPF/DMARC/CAA records, Certificate Transparency logs, and /.well-known/security.txt) with anti-SSRF safeguards blocking private and loopback IP ranges.

6. Data Retention & Data Subject Rights

Operational records, proof-of-work images, and audit logs are retained only as long as required for service auditability and contractual compliance. Authorized administrators and users may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data via security@bentengsiber.com.

7. Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (RFC 9116)

Security researchers who identify potential vulnerabilities within Benteng Siber services are encouraged to report findings responsibly via security@bentengsiber.com or consult our published RFC 9116 file at /.well-known/security.txt.
