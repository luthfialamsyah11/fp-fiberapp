Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Effective Date: 2026 • Compliant with Global Privacy Regulations & Google Play Content Policies

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to the Benteng Siber Ecosystem ("We", "Platform"). This document outlines our unwavering commitment to safeguarding privacy, informational integrity, and personal data rights across all ecosystem modules, including:

  • FiberOps FSM (Clientzone Admin & Technician PWA/Mobile) — Intelligent field workforce management platform for fiber optic network operators and telecommunication ISPs.
  • Benteng Siber Security Posture Scanner — Automated cyber threat assessment and public domain defense diagnostic engine.

By accessing, downloading, or authenticating into any of our portal gateways, you acknowledge and agree to the data handling practices described in this policy.

2. Data Classifications & Collection

We strictly limit collection to data essential for delivering high-reliability operational field management:

Category Data Elements Operational Purpose
Account Authentication Name, corporate email, cryptographically salted Bcrypt password hash, assigned role Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
GPS Telemetry Latitude & longitude coordinates captured during active service missions Fleet auto-dispatching, geofence confirmation, and route optimization
Proof of Work Before/After photographic documentation of fiber optical cabling, ONT units, timestamps SLA billing verification and anti-fraud quality control
Session State Encrypted Laravel Sanctum Bearer tokens Stateless, secure API authentication without credential caching

3. Mobile Device Permissions (Android & iOS)

The Technician mobile client requests specific runtime permissions solely for task execution:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Captures high-accuracy geolocation exclusively from mission start until mission completion. Tracking is automatically deactivated when technicians go offline or complete active tasks.
  • CAMERA: Utilized directly to record live, unedited photographic proof of physical network installations, bypassing deceptive third-party image manipulation.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Encrypted HTTPS/TLS transmission to the backend API gateway with offline caching resilience.

4. Processing Purposes & Commercial Safeguards

Data collected is utilized exclusively for enterprise telecommunication operations and network uptime optimization. We explicitly guarantee:

  • Zero data monetization, leasing, or selling to data brokers or third-party ad networks.
  • No background GPS tracking outside active shift duty or assigned mission lifecycles.
  • No cross-organizational data sharing without explicit legal or statutory authorization.

5. Data Protection Standards & Cryptography

We enforce Security by Design across all software architecture tiers:

  • Transport Encryption: 100% of network payloads are encrypted via TLS 1.3 enforced by HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS).
  • IDOR & API Guardrails: Every endpoint enforces strict resource-level authorization checks, preventing horizontal privilege escalation between field crews.
  • Password Hashing: Passwords are irreversibly hashed using standard industry bcrypt algorithms.
  • Dynamic Throttling: API rate-limiting filters actively mitigate brute-force and credential-stuffing attacks.

6. Security Posture Scanner Diagnostic Policies

For the Benteng Siber Security Scanner SaaS module:

  • Domain audits are executed in a passive, strictly non-intrusive manner (validating public SSL certificates, querying public DNSSEC records, and checking public HTTP security response headers).
  • Scan scores are stored securely to benchmark historical security posture improvements.
  • We strictly do not perform invasive penetration attempts, denial-of-service tests, or malicious payload injections.

7. Data Subject Rights & Retention

Users hold full rights to request access, rectification, restriction, or complete erasure of their stored personal data. Account and telemetry deletion requests are verified and permanently executed within 14 business days.

8. Regulatory Compliance & RFC 9116 Standards

Our mobile distributions adhere to the Google Play Developer Content Policy regarding sensitive user data processing. Responsible vulnerability disclosures are maintained in compliance with the RFC 9116 standard at /.well-known/security.txt.
